Our Blouse Samples Are on the Way

Hi everyone! Sadie here with a quick update on Woven.

Over the past few weeks, my mom and I have been working with Plucky Reach, a company that helps new fashion brands take a design from concept to market. They connected us with a manufacturer and kept us updated through every stage of production.

The first samples of the Woven blouse are finished. A couple of weeks ago we saw photos of them for the first time, and it was so exciting to see the design we built from the Dorcas House survey turn into real clothing. The samples are shipping to us overnight and should arrive this weekend.

We also have some great news to share. When Janet Varguez, Plucky Reach's Sourcing and Production Director, learned that these blouses are going to the women of Dorcas House, she told us the cause is close to her heart. She worked with the manufacturer to lower the cost of each blouse, which means every dollar given to Woven will go further. Thank you, Janet, and thank you to Abby Perez and the whole Plucky Reach team for believing in this project.

Once the samples arrive, we'll try them on, check the fit and fabric, and take photos. We'll share those pictures here and with everyone on our email list, along with details on how to reserve a blouse.

Thank you for following along!

-Sadie
